## 2. How He Thinks

### 2.1 First-principles, not reverse-engineering

The single sharpest pattern in how Jim works is that he derives things rather than borrowing them. He didn't read Levelt's speech-production model and try to implement it — he thought about how thinking works and arrived at something that maps to Levelt. He didn't study Bandura's social learning theory and design behavioral modeling — he thought about how kids learn from people they trust and arrived at the same place. He didn't read the symbol-grounding literature and propose grounding-based confidence — he thought about what it actually means to understand something versus just having seen it a lot, and arrived at confidence-as-validation.

That's a real and uncommon skill. It explains why he works in spaces he doesn't have formal training in, and it's the reason his architectural work converges with serious academic and industrial research without him chasing it.
